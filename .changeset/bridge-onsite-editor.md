Editing on the site gets the on-site editor (`mountSiteEditor`, which `getContentBridge` mounts there in place of the prompt box): a panel docked on the right with the site reflowed beside it, Ask and Edit fields, the scope always shown, answers with a before and after that preview on the page until kept (Keep it, Try another, Undo), hovered blocks named on the page, and a launcher at the bottom left to minimize, hide the outlines and exit. Its words are `SiteEditorLabels` (`DEFAULT_SITE_EDITOR_LABELS`); a site's fixed elements make room for the panel with the `--obelism-dock` custom property or the `data-obelism-dock` attribute. On the site `bridge.environment` is now the session's, and `getState().undoSteps` says how many steps `undo()` can take back. `openForm` takes `onEscape`, for forms that report Escape. The prompt box's default words follow the redesign: Ask / Edit fields for Prompt / Form, Exit for Exit editing, and “Describe a change, like “make it warmer”” as the placeholder; it draws the Obelism mark as the design system does.
