Editing on the site: the entry form stays loaded. Switching to Ask and back, or minimising the panel, used to throw the form away and load it again — its own request, the model and the entry's fields — which is most of the wait before the fields show. It also opens sooner the first time: the bar asks for the entry and for the form's address together, and the form page learns in its address which entry it is for, so it loads that entry's fields while the bar hands over the content.
