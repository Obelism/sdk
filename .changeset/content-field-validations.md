`FieldDefinition` has Content's new field settings: `format` (`url`, `email`, `color` or `slug`) and `unique` on text fields, `pattern` on textareas, `unique` on numbers, `min` and `max` on multiple assets and references, and `defaultValue` on text, textarea, number, boolean and select fields. Typed models (`content model pull`, `content types`) can use them.
